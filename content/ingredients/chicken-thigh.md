tip: Start skin-side down in a medium pan and leave it alone so the fat renders and the skin crisps.

  handling:
    note: Don't rinse raw chicken; it spreads bacteria around the sink. After handling it, wash your hands with soap and warm water for 20 seconds, and wash anything it touched.

    note: Cook chicken until an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part reads 165°F / 74°C; colour isn't a reliable sign. Rest it on a clean plate, not the one that held it raw.

Thighs have more fat and connective tissue than breast meat, so they stay moist and forgive a few extra minutes of cooking. Bone-in, skin-on thighs bring the most flavour; boneless thighs cook faster.
