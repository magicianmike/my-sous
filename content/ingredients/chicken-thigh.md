---
name: Chicken thigh
category: meat
slotTypes: [protein]
tip: Start skin-side down in a medium pan and leave it alone so the fat renders and the skin crisps.
---
Thighs have more fat and connective tissue than breast meat, so they stay moist and forgive a few extra minutes of cooking. Bone-in, skin-on thighs bring the most flavour; boneless thighs cook faster.
