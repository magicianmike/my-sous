---
name: Garlic
category: allium
cuisines: [southern-italian, ligurian, cantonese, oaxacan]
slotTypes: [aromatic]
tip: Cook garlic gently; it turns bitter if it browns too far.
---
Raw garlic is sharp and pungent, and slow cooking makes it sweet and mellow. How you cut it changes its strength: crushed or grated garlic tastes stronger than sliced.
