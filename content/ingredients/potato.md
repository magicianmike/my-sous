culturalContext: Potato does opposite jobs in two kitchens. In Punjabi cooking, potato is the partner vegetable in aloo gobi and many curries; in Lebanon, it's fried for batata harra. In Naples, it's the base of pasta e patate and gnocchi alla sorrentina, and in Oaxaca, it simmers with other vegetables in moles such as chichilo and amarillo.
