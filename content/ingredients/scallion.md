---
name: Scallion
category: allium
cuisines: [cantonese]
slotTypes: [aromatic, garnish]
tip: Cook with the white parts and save the green tops to scatter on at the end.
---
Scallions, also called spring onions or green onions, are milder than onions. The whites cook like a gentle onion, and the greens are fresh and grassy.
